    The operating rules

    The Mutual Step Principle

    Invite → Notice → Ask → Match Pace

    Progress is based on reciprocal behaviour over time, not chemistry, urgency, obsession, or text intensity.

    Pattern Reciprocity Vector

    • Intentional choice
    • Frictionless depth
    • Symmetrical effort
    • Active memory
    • Mutual curiosity
    • Organic safety
    • Autonomy preservation

    Green — proceed naturally

    Consistent communication, equal initiation, emotional safety, and respect for a “no” or boundary.

    Yellow — pause and observe

    If you are carrying most of the contact/logistics, signals are mixed, or intensity is racing ahead of substance, stop accelerating and observe.

    Red — freeze progression

    Pressure after “no,” possessiveness, isolation attempts, humiliation, monitoring, constant anger, sexual or financial pressure, or punishment for boundaries freezes progression.

    Non-linear off-ramp

    Either person can slow down or step back. A slowdown is calibration, not proof of rejection. A red safety indicator is different: attraction or loneliness never overrides it.

    Privacy & control

    Your data stays in your browser

    This build has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no matching database, and no server-side relationship record. Your entries are stored in this browser using local storage.

    Important: browser storage is not the same as encrypted storage. Anyone who can open this browser profile on an unlocked device may be able to view the app. Use your device lock and do not enter information you would be unsafe storing on the device.

    The app never publishes loneliness, living situation, abuse history, income, budget, or isolation information. It does not show “people alone near you” and it does not use private vulnerability data for matching.
